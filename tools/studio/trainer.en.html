All in English. Only readings that speech recognition confirms as English go into training, and every model is graded on an English exam: texts it never heard while training, built on r/l, th and v/w. The content encoder is chosen among those licensed for commercial use; multilingual encoders with non-commercial licences (mHuBERT-147, MMS) are not offered. This is your own trial studio, kept apart from Quetzaly's Spanish studio.

In the cloud: your readings (already checked as English) are uploaded to a private Hugging Face repository, trained on an L40S, and the epochs come back to this PC, where the English exam runs. Billed per minute of real use; the page shows the cost while it runs.
A version is saved every N epochs and each one takes the English exam: the one that is understood best wins, not necessarily the last (that avoids over-training). You can close this page; training keeps going. While it trains, the other studio's renders and auto-flow wait (they share the GPU).
