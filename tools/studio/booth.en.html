How to wear the lavalier
• Center of the chest, a hand's width below your chin (15–20 cm), mic head pointing up.
• Outside your clothing: under fabric the voice turns dull and you hear rubbing.
• Nothing touching it: necklaces, collar, hair, your hand. Every brush is recorded.
• Make a small loop of cable under the clip and tape it to your shirt so the cable doesn't tug or rustle.
• While reading, keep your head still; the mic stays put, your mouth moves.
